      How to Play

      Goal

      Survive as long as possible, score by wrecking targets, and escape escalating police pressure.

      Controls

      • Left/Right or gamepad stick steer.
      • Up accelerates. Down brakes.
      • Space or gamepad south button uses turbo when available.
      • Escape pauses.

      Audio Driving

      • Your engine pans left or right when you drift from the center. Keep it centered.
      • Curve warnings play from the turn direction about two seconds early.
      • Target beacons point toward pedestrians, bars, turbo, and armor.
      • Cop engines and sirens grow behind you as they close in.

      Status Keys

      • F1 reads score, health, heat, speed, cops, and armor.
      • F2 reads nearest target.
      • F3 reads current or upcoming curve.
      • F4 reads turbo and armor.
      • F5 reads lane position.
