    You are at the pizza shop.

      Press 1–9 to re-read a pizza, M to hear the city map. Space to start driving.

      How to Play

      Controls

      • Up / Down — accelerate / brake.
      • Left / Right — turn at the next crossing.
      • 1…9 — pick a pizza from the bag.
      • Space — hand over the selected pizza.
      • Enter — cancel a queued turn.
      • Esc — pause.

      Status Hotkeys

      • F1 — next GPS instruction.
      • F2 — the pizza in your hand.
      • F3 — distance and direction to the pizza shop.
      • F4 — time and tip total.
      • F5 — where you are.
      • F6 — the city map.
      • F7 — the crossing ahead and its light.

      What you hear

      A bell rings at the crossing where your next turn is.

      Objective

      Memorise which pizza goes where, deliver each one, and head back for the next job.

      The city

      Six streets each way, 100 metres apart. New landmarks every run.

      Tap each cue to hear it on its own.
