# Signal Heist GDD

Signal Heist is an audio-first stealth score chase. The player is an operator moving through continuous top-down 2D contracts with a player-locked listener: front is always where the operator is facing.

## Core Loop

1. Enter a contract.
2. Locate and steal every data cache by following spatial tones.
3. Avoid guards, camera sweeps, trace pressure, and noise mistakes.
4. Reach the exit beacon.
5. Repeat through harder contracts until capture.

## Scoring

- Data cache: immediate points, increasing by contract.
- Contract clear: large bonus plus time remaining.
- Perfect contract: extra bonus for no detection and no jammer use.
- Ghost pass: pass close to a guard or camera without being seen.
- Jammer use: useful, but applies a score penalty.

## Accessibility

- The game must be playable by ear.
- Guards, cameras, caches, and the exit all have spatial cues.
- Dynamic music communicates detection pressure.
- F1-F7 read score, contract, objective, threat, heat, tools, and position.
- Learn Sounds auditions the major cues.

## Audio Direction

Modern dark/horror synthesis, not retro arcade beeps. Low drones establish dread. Heartbeat, filtered noise, and risers scale with detection. Clean ghost passes briefly open the harmony so success is musical and readable.
